# Windwaker Dotfiles
A very minimalistic and experimental-ish rice. Widgets built with `eww`, wallpaper and stuff available in `assets`, some other theming options here and there, and also my `niri` config as well as `lrcsnc` stuff ([my creation](https://github.com/Endg4meZer0/lrcsnc)!!!)

## Why "Windwaker"?
One of my all-time favorite metalcore bands that I listen to is [Windwaker](https://youtu.be/HlIb9sXTEXo), and *their* name, in turn, was inspired by The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker game. And also wind kinda correlates with the minty green aesthetic I'm looking for. That's pretty much it.

By the way, the VSCode theme is available in Marketplace (both open and Microsoft's marketplace) as `Windwaker`.
